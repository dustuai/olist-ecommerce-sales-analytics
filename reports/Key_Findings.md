## Delivery Performance

- Average delivery time: 12.5 days
- Late delivery rate: 8.1%

## Payment Behavior

- Most frequently used payment method: Credit_Card

## Customer Satisfaction

- Average review score: 4.09 / 5
- Highest-rated category: Health_Beauty

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Delivery Performance

Focus logistics improvement efforts on regions with consistently high delivery times or late-delivery rates.

### 2. Optimize Category Strategy

Prioritize high-performing categories for inventory planning and marketing decisions.

### 3. Monitor Seller Performance

Identify sellers with consistently high late-delivery rates and investigate operational causes.

### 4. Improve Customer Experience

Monitor the relationship between delivery performance and review scores to identify areas where logistics improvements may improve customer satisfaction.
